# 3-Minute Recruiter Demo

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem

> "MoneyFlow is designed around a decision-support problem: transaction visibility doesn't automatically tell users what to do next."

Show the dashboard.

## 0:30–1:00 — Insight

Point to:
- cash flow
- spending categories
- "What changed?"

Say:

> "The product deliberately limits the first screen to a few actionable insights instead of turning into a dashboard full of charts."

## 1:00–1:30 — User action

Click:
**Review transactions**

Then open Transactions.

Explain:

> "Users can correct categorization rather than blindly trusting automation."

## 1:30–2:00 — Planning

Open Plan.

Change a category budget.

Open Goal.

Explain:

> "The MVP closes the loop from understanding to planning."

## 2:00–2:30 — Metrics

Open Product Metrics.

Explain:

> "The North Star is Weekly Actionable Financial Check-ins. In the prototype, I instrument meaningful actions rather than treating page views as value."

## 2:30–3:00 — PM trade-off

Say:

> "I intentionally did not build bank integrations, lending, investment advice, or autonomous transfers in the MVP. The product question is whether users value the decision-support loop before adding expensive and higher-risk infrastructure."
