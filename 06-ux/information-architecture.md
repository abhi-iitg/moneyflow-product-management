# Information Architecture

```text
MoneyFlow
├── Home / Weekly Check-in
│   ├── Current position
│   ├── What changed
│   └── Next actions
├── Transactions
│   ├── All
│   ├── Needs review
│   └── Categories
├── Plan
│   ├── Budget
│   ├── Savings goal
│   └── Recurring expenses
├── Insights
│   ├── Spending patterns
│   └── Monthly review
└── Settings
    ├── Data & privacy
    ├── Export
    └── Delete
```

## Navigation principle

The first screen should answer:

1. Where am I?
2. What changed?
3. What should I do?
