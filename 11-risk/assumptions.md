# Assumptions

| Assumption | Confidence | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Users want simpler financial check-ins | Medium | interviews/survey |
| Manual import is acceptable for MVP | Low | prototype test |
| Users trust explainable prompts more | Medium | usability experiment |
| Weekly is an appropriate cadence | Low | behavior research |
| Users will pay for advanced planning | Low | pricing research |
| Privacy controls improve adoption | Medium | E01 experiment |
| Safe-to-spend is understandable | Low | usability test |

## Assumption rule

A high-impact, low-confidence assumption must be validated before becoming a roadmap dependency.
