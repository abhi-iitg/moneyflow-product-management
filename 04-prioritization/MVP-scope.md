# MVP Scope

## MVP goal

Validate whether users repeatedly use MoneyFlow to understand and act on their monthly finances.

## In scope

- synthetic/demo transaction import
- transaction categorization
- manual correction
- cash-flow summary
- recurring-expense identification
- flexible budget setup
- one savings goal
- weekly check-in
- explainable prompts
- privacy/data controls
- product analytics instrumentation

## Out of scope

- live bank credential aggregation
- payment initiation
- lending
- credit decisions
- investment advice
- tax advice
- autonomous transfers
- social feed
- marketplace
- financial advisor replacement

## MVP success gate

Advance to integration and personalization only if:

- activation ≥ 45%
- week-4 return ≥ 35%
- weekly actionable check-in rate ≥ 45%
- severe trust/privacy incidents = 0
- category correction rate trends downward over repeated imports

Targets are hypotheses to validate, not achieved results.
