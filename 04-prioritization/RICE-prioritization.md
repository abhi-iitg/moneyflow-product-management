# RICE Prioritization

## Formula

**RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort**

Reach = expected users affected per quarter  
Impact = 3 massive, 2 high, 1 medium, 0.5 low, 0.25 minimal  
Confidence = 0–100%  
Effort = person-months

## Decision

Top MVP work should establish the basic value loop:

**Import → Understand → Act → Return**

RICE is a decision aid, not an automatic roadmap. Strategic risk and dependencies can override numerical rank.
