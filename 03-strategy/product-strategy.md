# Product Strategy

## Strategy thesis

The first version should solve a narrow, high-frequency job better than a broad "all-in-one finance app."

### Phase 1 — Understand

- import
- categorize
- cash-flow snapshot
- recurring commitments

### Phase 2 — Plan

- flexible budgets
- savings goals
- safe-to-spend estimate

### Phase 3 — Act

- contextual prompts
- scenario planning
- habit loops

### Phase 4 — Personalize

- user-specific patterns
- adaptive recommendations
- richer financial planning

## Strategic trade-offs

### We choose
- narrow MVP
- manual import initially
- explainable rules
- privacy controls
- measurable actions

### We reject
- instant bank integrations in MVP
- investment recommendations
- social feeds
- gamification for its own sake
- AI chatbot as the primary experience

## Why manual import first?

It reduces integration complexity and allows validation of the **core product value** before paying the cost of financial-data connectivity.

This is a product sequencing decision, not a claim that manual import is the best long-term experience.
