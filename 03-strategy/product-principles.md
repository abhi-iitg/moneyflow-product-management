# Product Principles

1. **Trust before convenience** — explain data use before asking for data.
2. **Action before analytics** — every important insight should have a next step.
3. **Explain before recommend** — users should understand why an insight appears.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — show the minimum needed first.
5. **Flexible, not punitive** — budgets should adapt to real life.
6. **Privacy is a feature** — make controls visible, not hidden.
7. **No fake certainty** — distinguish estimates from facts.
8. **Instrument the behavior** — every core hypothesis must be measurable.
9. **Build the wedge first** — avoid all-in-one finance-app scope.
10. **Design for learning** — every release should produce evidence for the next decision.
