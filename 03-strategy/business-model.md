# Business Model

## Model

**Freemium**

### Free
- transaction import
- basic categorization
- monthly cash-flow
- one savings goal
- weekly check-in

### Premium
- advanced planning
- multiple goals
- scenario planning
- custom rules
- deeper historical analysis
- household/shared planning

## Monetization principle

Never monetize by selling personal financial data.

## Pricing hypothesis

Illustrative hypothesis:

**₹149/month or ₹1,199/year**

Price should be validated with willingness-to-pay research and value-based testing.

## Business model risks

- low willingness to pay
- high churn
- trust barrier
- expensive acquisition
- support cost
- integration cost

## Monetization gate

Do not optimize aggressively for paid conversion until users demonstrate repeated value through the core weekly check-in behavior.
