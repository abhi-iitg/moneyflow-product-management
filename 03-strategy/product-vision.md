# Product Vision

## Vision

> **Make everyday financial decisions simpler, calmer, and more explainable.**

## 3-year aspiration

MoneyFlow becomes a trusted weekly financial check-in for young adults.

The product evolves from:

**transaction understanding**
→ **planning**
→ **personalized decision support**

without crossing into autonomous financial decisions.

## Mission

Reduce the cognitive effort required to understand personal cash flow and take a useful next step.

## Strategic pillars

1. Trust first
2. Action over information
3. Explainability by default
4. Low-maintenance habits
5. Progressive personalization
