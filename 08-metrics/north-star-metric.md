# North Star Metric

## Weekly Actionable Financial Check-ins (WAFC)

A user counts once per week when they:

1. view their current financial status, AND
2. complete at least one meaningful action.

Examples:
- correct a category
- review a recurring expense
- create/update a budget
- update a savings goal
- acknowledge an important spending insight

## Why this metric?

### Too shallow
DAU can reward passive checking.

### Too narrow
Budget creation alone ignores understanding.

### Too commercial
Premium conversion is too far downstream for an MVP value metric.

### WAFC
Connects:
**understanding + action + repeat behavior**

## Metric guardrails

WAFC must be monitored with:
- privacy incidents
- false/incorrect insight reports
- deletion failures
- import failure rate

A rising WAFC with deteriorating trust is not success.
