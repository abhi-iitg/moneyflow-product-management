# Jobs To Be Done

## Core Job

> **When I am approaching or moving through a month, help me understand whether my current spending is sustainable and what action I should take next, so I can stay in control without manually analyzing every transaction.**

## Functional jobs

- categorize transactions
- see income vs expenses
- identify recurring commitments
- estimate discretionary room
- track savings goals
- adjust budgets

## Emotional jobs

- feel in control
- reduce financial anxiety caused by uncertainty
- avoid shame from "failed budgets"
- feel confident making everyday spending decisions

## Social jobs

- appear financially responsible
- avoid repeatedly asking others for money
- make shared-expense conversations easier

## Job hierarchy

```text
Understand financial position
        ↓
Identify important change
        ↓
Choose action
        ↓
Execute action
        ↓
Observe outcome
```

## Product principle

Every major insight should answer:

**"So what?"**

If a chart cannot lead to a useful decision, it should not be prioritized for MVP.
