# User Research Plan

## Objective

Validate whether the core problem is sufficiently painful and identify the smallest product behavior that creates repeat value.

## Research questions

1. How do users currently monitor monthly spending?
2. Which financial decisions cause the most uncertainty?
3. Which information do they trust?
4. How often do they review transactions?
5. What makes budgeting tools feel burdensome?
6. What causes users to abandon financial-management products?
7. Which notifications are helpful vs intrusive?
8. What privacy concerns prevent users from importing financial data?
9. Which actions would users realistically take inside the product?
10. What would make them return weekly?

## Qualitative plan

**Participants:** 12–15 target users

**Mix:**
- 6 students
- 6 early-career professionals
- 2–3 users with an existing budgeting habit

**Method:** 30-minute semi-structured interviews

### Interview guide

- "Walk me through the last time you checked your finances."
- "What were you trying to decide?"
- "What information did you need?"
- "What was difficult?"
- "What did you do next?"
- "Tell me about a time a budgeting tool annoyed you."
- "How do you handle recurring expenses?"
- "What would make you trust a new financial product?"
- "What information would you never want a product to store?"
- "If the product could answer one financial question instantly, what would it be?"

## Quantitative validation

After qualitative synthesis, run a survey with 100+ target users.

Measure:
- frequency of financial check-ins
- current tools
- budgeting pain
- recurring-expense awareness
- savings-goal behavior
- willingness to try
- privacy concerns
- willingness to pay

## Decision rule

Proceed to MVP if:

- ≥ 60% report the problem at least monthly
- ≥ 40% describe current solutions as inconvenient/incomplete
- at least 3 recurring high-severity pain themes appear across interviews
- at least one repeatable job emerges with a clear action opportunity

These are **validation thresholds**, not claimed research results.

## Research ethics

Do not collect:
- bank credentials
- account numbers
- card numbers
- OTPs
- real transaction exports without explicit consent and secure handling

Use synthetic data for demonstrations.
