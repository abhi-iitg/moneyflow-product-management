# Opportunity Solution Tree

## Desired outcome

**Increase recurring financial-control behavior among target users.**

### Opportunity 1 — Users do not understand current position

Solutions:
- monthly cash-flow snapshot
- spending trend
- recurring commitments
- "what changed?" summary

### Opportunity 2 — Users do not know what to do next

Solutions:
- actionable prompts
- safe-to-spend guidance
- budget adjustment suggestions
- savings-goal nudges

### Opportunity 3 — Budgeting feels burdensome

Solutions:
- flexible category limits
- auto-suggested budgets
- one-tap corrections
- exception-based review

### Opportunity 4 — Users distrust financial-data products

Solutions:
- privacy-first onboarding
- transparent data map
- delete/export controls
- no credential storage in MVP
- explicit explanation of what is and is not analyzed

### Opportunity 5 — Users do not return

Solutions:
- weekly check-in
- progress toward goal
- recurring-expense reminders
- "since last check-in" summary

## Chosen MVP opportunity

**Opportunity 1 + 2**

Reason: highest direct connection to the core product promise and lowest dependency on complex integrations.
