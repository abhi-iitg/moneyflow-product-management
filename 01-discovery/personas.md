# Personas

## Primary Persona — Early-Career Planner

**Name:** Aanya  
**Age:** 23  
**Stage:** First 1–2 years of full-time work  
**Behavior:** Uses banking and payment apps frequently; checks balance but rarely reviews spending systematically.

### Goals
- avoid overspending before payday
- build an emergency fund
- understand recurring commitments
- save for near-term goals

### Frustrations
- too many transactions
- categories are sometimes wrong
- dashboards show information but not priorities
- budgeting feels like another task

### Trigger
Unexpectedly reaching the end of the month with less discretionary money than expected.

### Desired outcome
A quick answer to:
> "What can I safely spend, and what should I change?"

---

## Secondary Persona — College Student

**Name:** Rohan  
**Age:** 20  
**Stage:** Undergraduate student  
**Behavior:** Irregular income/support, high discretionary-spend variability.

### Goals
- control food/entertainment spending
- save for travel/equipment
- avoid asking family for additional money

### Frustrations
- fixed budgets don't fit irregular months
- financial apps can feel too serious or complicated

### Product implication

The experience should support **flexible planning**, not only fixed monthly budgets.

---

## Anti-Persona

**Professional investor/trader**

MoneyFlow is not designed to optimize trading, portfolio allocation or investment decisions.

This prevents scope creep and reduces regulatory/product-risk complexity.
