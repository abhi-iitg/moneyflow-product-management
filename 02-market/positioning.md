# Positioning

## Positioning statement

For students and early-career professionals who want better control of everyday spending, **MoneyFlow** is a privacy-first personal finance companion that converts transaction history into clear, explainable actions.

Unlike traditional dashboards and manual spreadsheets, MoneyFlow focuses on the **next best financial action**, not just displaying more information.

## Category

**Action-oriented personal financial management**

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** Know what to do next.
2. **Proof:** Clear cash-flow and spending context.
3. **Trust:** Transparent, privacy-first data handling.
4. **Ease:** Minimal setup and low-maintenance planning.

## Anti-positioning

We are not:
- a trading platform
- a lender
- a credit-score product
- a tax filing service
- a robo-advisor
- an autonomous money-moving agent
