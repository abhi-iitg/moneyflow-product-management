# Market Sizing

## Purpose

Avoid fake precision. This case study uses a transparent scenario model rather than presenting unsupported market numbers as facts.

## Bottom-up model

Let:

- **N** = addressable target users
- **A** = annual active adoption rate
- **P** = annual premium conversion rate
- **ARPP** = annual premium revenue per paying user

Then:

**Annual Revenue = N × A × P × ARPP**

## Illustrative scenario

Assumption set for strategy discussion:

| Variable | Conservative | Base | Upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Addressable users | 1,000,000 | 2,000,000 | 4,000,000 |
| Active adoption | 5% | 8% | 12% |
| Premium conversion | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| Annual premium value | ₹1,200 | ₹1,800 | ₹2,400 |

These are **scenario assumptions, not market facts**.

### Base scenario

2,000,000 × 8% × 5% × ₹1,800 = **₹14.4 crore annualized revenue scenario**

The purpose is to understand sensitivity, not to forecast actual revenue.

## Key learning

The largest strategic uncertainty is not price. It is whether MoneyFlow creates enough recurring value to earn habit and paid conversion.

Therefore MVP should optimize for **retention and value realization before monetization**.
