# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target beachhead

Students and early-career professionals in digitally active communities.

## Acquisition channels

### 1. Campus communities
Message:
> "Know where your money goes before the month gets away from you."

### 2. Creator/content
Content themes:
- monthly money reset
- recurring-expense audit
- safe-to-spend planning
- financial check-in template

### 3. Referral
Invite a friend after a user completes their first valuable check-in.

### 4. Product-led loop
Shareable, privacy-safe monthly planning templates — never share private transaction data.

## Launch positioning

Avoid:
> "AI financial advisor"

Use:
> "Your weekly financial check-in."

## Growth hypothesis

If the first check-in produces a clear action in under five minutes, users will be more likely to return and refer.

## Channel experiment

Test:
- problem-led messaging
- outcome-led messaging
- privacy-led messaging

Primary metric:
activated users per qualified visitor

Guardrail:
low-quality signups / support burden
